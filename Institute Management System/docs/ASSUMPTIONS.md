# Assumptions

Decisions taken where the master prompt left room for interpretation. Each can be revisited.

## Platform and deployment
1. Single deployable modular monolith (FastAPI) + one Celery worker running beat; horizontal scaling
   of the worker requires moving beat to a dedicated service.
2. TLS is terminated outside the bundled nginx (cloud LB / host proxy). The bundled nginx speaks HTTP.
3. The project lives at the workspace root (no extra sub-folder).
4. Initial schema is a single Alembic baseline (`0001_initial`) that materialises the model metadata
   and adds integrity triggers; every later change must be a new revision.
5. No demo or sample data is seeded. `migrate` seeds only permissions and system roles;
   `create-institute` creates the institute, a main branch, default settings and the first admin.
6. Python dependencies are pinned in `requirements.txt`; the frontend lock file is generated by
   `npm install` (committed `package-lock.json` when present) and images build with `npm ci` / `npm install`.
7. Times are stored in UTC; dates/times shown in the institute timezone (default Asia/Kolkata).
   Class schedules are wall-clock times in the institute timezone.
8. Default currency INR; amounts are `NUMERIC(12,2)`. Multi-currency within one institute is not supported.
9. Financial year starts in April (configurable month on the institute); document numbers
   (invoice, receipt, refund, credit note, payslip) are sequential per institute per financial year
   with configurable prefixes, generated with a row lock (no gaps from concurrent requests;
   cancelled documents keep their number).

## Tenancy and access
10. Every business record belongs to one institute (`institute_id`); SaaS multi-tenancy is a shared
    database with row-level scoping enforced in the service layer plus cross-tenant FK validation.
    PostgreSQL RLS is not enabled.
11. Branch-scoped roles see only their assigned branches; institute-wide roles see all branches and
    may filter with the branch selector.
12. Teachers see students/sessions/materials of batches they are assigned to (teacher assignments
    or session teachers, including substitutes for that session).
13. Parents see only children linked through `student_parents` with portal access enabled.
14. Platform super-users (`is_superuser`) administer institutes/plans and can act inside an
    institute via `X-Institute-Id`; they are not part of any institute's role set.

## Academic operations
15. Student attendance statuses: present, absent, late, excused, approved_leave (teacher: present,
    absent, late, on_leave, excused). Attendance is locked on submission
    (`attendance.lock_on_submit`); changes require a correction request or the `attendance.reopen`
    permission, and every change is written to `attendance_audit_logs`.
16. A session is "completed" when the teacher completes it or attendance is submitted; only
    completed sessions count towards per-class billing and teacher compensation.
17. Sessions are generated 21 days ahead nightly (and on demand); generation is idempotent and skips
    holidays and schedule exceptions.
18. Per-class / per-hour teacher pay is generated only from **verified** teacher attendance
    (present/late) on completed sessions, so a substitute is paid for the sessions they actually
    took and an absent scheduled teacher is not paid for that session.
19. Exam grading: objective questions auto-graded (exact/option match; numeric with tolerance where
    configured); descriptive answers require manual grading. Results are visible to students/parents
    only after approval and publication.
20. Material and submission versions are immutable; a new upload creates a new version and the
    previous ones remain downloadable to staff.

## Finance
21. Per-class billing charges students for sessions whose attendance status is in
    `billing.billable_attendance_statuses` (default present and late). Charges are reviewed before
    being invoiced.
22. `per_subject` and `per_course` agreements are invoiced on the monthly cycle using the agreed
    amount; `quarterly`/`annual` on their period; `installment` from fee-plan items; `one_time` once.
23. Payments are allocated FIFO to the oldest open invoices unless the cashier allocates manually;
    over-payments remain as unallocated credit on the payment.
24. Posted invoices/payments are never edited or deleted: corrections are credit notes,
    supplementary invoices, payment reversals and refunds. Enforced by DB triggers.
25. Late fees are a fixed amount per fee plan, applied by the nightly job after the grace days as
    a supplementary invoice linked to the overdue invoice, at most once per invoice.
26. Tax (GST) is a percentage per invoice line (default from the institute/tax-rate master); no
    e-invoicing/IRN integration.
27. Accounting is a single-entry cash/expense book with a student ledger, cashbook and bank
    register, budgets and financial-year close — not a full double-entry general ledger.
28. Teacher compensation is computed per payroll period; compensation types are fixed_monthly
    (prorated for joining/exit inside the period), per_class, per_hour and custom (manual payable
    entries). Rules can be specific to a subject/batch. Statutory deductions (PF/ESI/TDS) are
    entered as manual adjustments, not calculated.

## Integrations and AI
29. Providers implemented: SMTP, MSG91, Twilio, Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, Web Push (VAPID),
    Razorpay, OpenAI-compatible chat completions, Zoom, Google Meet. Other gateways/providers need
    new adapters.
30. Without a configured provider the feature is unavailable and clearly marked; nothing is simulated.
31. AI question generation works from pasted text or text extracted from uploaded text-based
    documents; OCR of scanned images/PDFs is not included. All AI output is a draft requiring review.
32. Razorpay refunds are recorded in the ERP but executed in the Razorpay dashboard.

## Data and privacy
33. Retention: login history 365 days, notifications 180 days, audit logs kept forever (configurable).
    Financial and academic records are never purged automatically.
34. Account-closure requests are recorded for administrator review; personal data is not
    automatically erased because statutory records must be retained.
35. Backups are local to the Docker host (`backup_data` volume); off-site copying is the operator's
    responsibility.
